What makes the concept different

This page is built around one simple idea: the product should be nutritionally useful, commercially legible, and ingredient-clean enough that a shopper scanning it in Yuka gets a reassuring result instead of a wall of warnings.

Short ingredient deck

The formulation stays close to recognisable dairy ingredients, with protein added through whey and milk protein concentrates rather than through a long list of texturisers or flavour systems.

Retail-friendly story

It reads as premium Irish-style dairy, not sports nutrition. That matters because the product needs to feel trustworthy for older adults and still attractive for general health-conscious shoppers.

Built for scoring logic

The concept aims to stack the deck in favour of Yuka by supporting the nutritional side, avoiding additive penalties, and using an organic pathway where commercially feasible.

Ingredient list

The product is designed around ingredients that sound like food, not lab shorthand. That keeps the label readable and gives you room to talk about quality without getting lost in additive clean-up.

  • Organic whole milk — the main dairy base for body, flavour, and familiarity.
  • Organic skim milk — helps lift protein while keeping fat moderated.
  • Organic whey protein concentrate — the main high-quality protein input.
  • Organic milk protein concentrate — rounds out texture and protein density.
  • Organic dulce de leche caramel — made from organic sugar, organic butter, organic cream, and sea salt.
  • Organic vanilla extract — supports a warm dessert profile without artificial flavouring.
  • Live probiotic cultures — optional functional addition for a more modern healthy-ageing proposition.

Label-ready line

Ingredients: Organic whole milk, organic skim milk, organic whey protein concentrate, organic milk protein concentrate, organic dulce de leche caramel (organic sugar, organic butter, organic cream, sea salt), organic vanilla extract, live probiotic cultures.

What stays out

  • No carrageenan, gelatin, pectin, or modified starch.
  • No sucralose, aspartame, or acesulfame K.
  • No artificial colours, preservatives, or flavour boosters.

Yuka positioning

The formulation is intentionally built to support a high Yuka score by leaning on three levers: solid nutritional quality, a zero-additive stance, and an organic-oriented sourcing path. It is not a guaranteed score, because final results depend on barcode data and final market-ready nutrition, but the concept is designed to aim for the top tier.

Why the concept should perform well

  • High protein per serving supports the nutritional side of the score.
  • Sugar is moderated instead of pushed into dessert territory.
  • Salt stays low and the formula avoids harsh processing cues on pack.
  • The additive list is intentionally empty, which is a big deal in scan-based shopper apps.
  • An organic-certified route gives the concept a useful finishing bump.
